KaTeX — vendored copy for offline rendering of the formulas in this card
=========================================================================

Package:  katex 0.16.9  (https://katex.org/)
Files:    katex.min.js · katex.min.css · contrib/auto-render.min.js · fonts/ (woff2/woff/ttf)
Why:      the card must render with no network (no CDN), so the library and its fonts
